Caveat, stated because it matters: pure radial is the worst case.
Experienced electricians loop lighting points in and out, which
already recovers part of the topology saving. Real practice sits
between radial and optimal, so the topology figure should be read
as an upper bound, and the board siting figure as the clean result
since it holds the topology constant.
