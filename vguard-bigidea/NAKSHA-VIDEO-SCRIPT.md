# NAKSHA — 40 Second Video Pitch

The template asks for a **40 second** video pitch. That is roughly **100 spoken words**.
Show `NAKSHA-Diagram.pdf` full screen for the whole 40 seconds.

---

## The script (say exactly this)

> Indian homes are wired from memory. No drawing, no record, and forty-two percent of building fires here are electrical.
>
> NAKSHA takes the architect's plan and generates the whole electrical design against the national wiring codes.
>
> Three outputs. An exact wire schedule in metres. Phone AR marking, so the conduits go where the design says. And a permanent map of every cable in the house.
>
> Now here is the business. Every Indian wire brand already has an electrician app. All of them just hand out loyalty points. Nobody has put a tool in that channel that does actual work.
>
> And every design NAKSHA produces is a purchase order for V-Guard wire.

**104 words. About 40 seconds at a normal speaking pace.**

## Recording notes

- Open `NAKSHA-Diagram.pdf` **full screen** and screen record. One image, no slide changes.
- Move your cursor to each of the three output boxes as you name them.
- **Do a few takes.** 40 seconds is short enough that you can nail it in five minutes.
- Do not rush. If you overrun slightly, cut the sentence *"Phone AR marking, so the conduits go where the design says"* first. It is the least load bearing.
- The last line is the one they should remember. **Land it cleanly, then stop talking.**
- Export **1080p MP4**, upload to Google Drive, set sharing so anyone with the link can view, then paste that link on the cover page.
